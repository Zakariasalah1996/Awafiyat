Refund Policy

Last Updated: April 14, 2026

Overview

We want you to be satisfied with your Awafiyat subscription. If you are not satisfied, we offer a clear and fair refund policy.

1. Refund Period

✅ Full refund within 7 days of subscription

If you are not satisfied with your subscription, you can request a full refund within the first 7 days of your subscription.

2. Refund Conditions

You can get a refund if:

  • ✅ You request the refund within 7 days of subscription
  • ✅ You have not used the advanced features extensively
  • ✅ There were no technical issues on your end

Refunds will not be issued if:

  • ❌ More than 7 days have passed since your subscription
  • ❌ You could not use the application due to issues with your device
  • ❌ You canceled the subscription after more than 7 days
  • ❌ You were using a free version of the application

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, follow these steps:

  1. Through the app: Go to "My Account" → "Subscriptions" → "Request Refund"
  2. Via email: Send an email to support@awafiyat.manus.space with:
    • Your account number
    • Subscription date
    • Reason for refund request

4. Refund Processing

Stage Expected Time Notes
Submit refund request Immediate Request is submitted directly
Review request 24-48 hours We verify the eligibility of the request
Approve refund 1-3 business days We send approval confirmation
Process refund 3-7 business days Depends on your bank

5. Refund Methods

Refunds will be issued using the same method you used to pay:

  • 💳 Google Play: Direct refund to your Google account
  • 🍎 Apple App Store: Direct refund to your Apple account
  • 💰 Credit Card: Refund to the original card

6. Recurring Subscriptions

If you have a recurring subscription (monthly or yearly), you can:

  • ✅ Cancel your subscription at any time
  • ✅ Request a refund for the first payment within 7 days
  • ✅ No additional charges will be applied after cancellation

7. Annual Subscriptions

For annual subscriptions:

  • ✅ Full refund within 7 days of payment
  • ✅ After 7 days, no refunds are available
  • ✅ You can cancel the subscription but will not receive a refund

8. Exceptions

Refunds cannot be issued in the following cases:

  • ❌ Free subscriptions
  • ❌ Money earned from coupons or gifts
  • ❌ Subscriptions purchased from stores other than Google Play and App Store
  • ❌ Subscriptions purchased through illegal means

9. Price Updates

If the price changes after your subscription:

  • ✅ You will be notified of the new price before renewal
  • ✅ You can cancel your subscription before renewal
  • ✅ If you accept the new price, you cannot request a refund

10. Support and Assistance

If you encounter issues with your subscription or refund request:

Contact us:

📧 Email: support@awafiyat.manus.space

⏰ Response time: Within 24 hours

🌍 Countries served: Iraq, Saudi Arabia, United Arab Emirates

11. Privacy and Data

When you request a refund, we may retain some data for legal and accounting purposes. See our Privacy Policy for more information.

12. Changes to Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. You will be notified of any significant changes via email.
