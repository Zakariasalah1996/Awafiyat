Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 14, 2026

Introduction

The "Awafiyat" application (hereinafter referred to as the "Application") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting user data in accordance with applicable laws. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Data We Collect

a) Data You Provide Directly:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Age and gender
  • Health information (chronic diseases, allergies)
  • Family member information
  • Payment information (subscription details)

b) Data We Collect Automatically:

  • Unique device identifier
  • Device type and operating system
  • Application version
  • Usage history and features used
  • IP address
  • Geographic location information (if permitted)

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Provide and improve application services
  • Personalize user experience (suitable recipes, recommendations)
  • Send notifications and reminders
  • Process subscriptions and payments
  • Analyze performance and improve the application
  • Communicate with you about updates and offers
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Protection

We take strict security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, and modification:

  • Data encryption during transmission (SSL/TLS)
  • Secure storage on protected servers
  • Continuous security monitoring
  • Strict policies for our staff
  • No sharing of personal data with third parties without your consent

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties, except in the following cases:

  • Payment Processors: We share payment information with trusted payment processors (Google Play, Apple)
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose data if required by law
  • Harm Prevention: We may disclose data to protect our rights or user safety
  • Service Providers: We may use trusted service providers (hosting, analytics)

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data
  • Object to data processing
  • Transfer your data to another service
  • Withdraw consent at any time

6. Cookies

The application may use cookies or similar technologies to improve your experience. You can control these through your device settings.

7. Children

The application is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not intentionally collect data from children. If we discover that we have collected data from a child, we will delete it immediately.

8. Policy Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes via email or notification in the application.
